# Example prompts

These prompts show the range of briefs the skill can handle. They are examples, not fixed templates.

## Bangla explainer

> Create a 20-second vertical Bangla motion-graphics explainer for a general audience about how a solar panel turns sunlight into electricity. Use only these facts: sunlight reaches photovoltaic cells; the cells produce electrical current; an inverter converts that current into usable household electricity. Give me a production-ready timed storyboard and a practical implementation/handoff plan. Do not add claims.

## Arabic-English poster

> Create a 7-second Arabic-English motion poster for an imaginary public science event. Exact Arabic headline: "العلم يفتح آفاقاً جديدة". Exact English line: "SCIENCE WEEK 2026". Use a dark navy background, a warm yellow accent, and no voiceover. Keep both lines legible and editable. Provide the animation plan and the most practical available handoff.

## Data-led animation

> Make a 12-second widescreen infographic using this fictional dataset only: Sample A = 12 units, Sample B = 17 units, Sample C = 23 units. Explain that the values increase across the samples, but do not imply why. Include a timed scene plan, labels, chart motion, and a reproducible output plan.

## Localized product motion

> Create a reusable 6-second UI feature animation for an imaginary app called Northstar. Brand rules: cobalt blue and cream, rounded geometric type, calm pacing, no gradients. Write the on-screen text and captions in Spanish. Keep the UI clearly illustrative, provide an editable handoff plan, and preserve the design in this revision: “Keep the opening, make the feature reveal clearer, and give the end card one extra second.”
